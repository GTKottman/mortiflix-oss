# Mortiflix v3 video

A 30 to 120 second motion design video. Same rules as the explainer pipeline (one idea, approved work reused, look before you send), with these steps:

`brief` -> `script` -> `voice` (narration with the chosen ElevenLabs voice, and the music plan, in one step) -> `style-frames` (the Look) -> `stills` (one per spoken line) -> `animatic` (Motion: scene 1 is built alone and checked first; cuts and transitions are decided here) -> `sound` (effects planned from the motion; new sounds only after the owner confirms credits) -> `artcraft` (the final, delivering).

Stages (`stage_set: v3`): Script, Voice & music, Look, Stills, Motion, Sound, Artcraft. Stills, sound and artcraft are done by the studio itself (src/quickv3.mjs), never a session, never a paid call.

Removed from the explainer: `music`, `transitions`, `build`, `final`.

Step details, skills and the checklist: read `pipelines/explainer/PIPELINE.md` and follow it for steps that exist here. Ignore its transitions, music, build and final rows. The intake keeps `source_url`: store it, never fetch it here.
